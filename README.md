# FAIRE OS

Faire is essentially a screensaver that doubles as a chalkboard canvas. As we share our prompts with Faire, it generates output that stays on the screensaver as widgets. Users do not even have to click into the computer to start engaging with the internet and AI. 

> One click = interact with Faire 

> Two clicks = exit screensaver

We could benefit from some guidance around allowing videos to play right from the screensaver, our heaviest hurdle as of now to getting Faire universally equipped to generate and reference any data in any form, right. on. the. screensaver.
